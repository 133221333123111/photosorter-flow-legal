Privacy Policy for PhotoSorter

Last updated: September 19, 2026

Home Terms of Use

PhotoSorter is developed and published by POMF. This Privacy Policy explains how PhotoSorter handles information when you use the app.

1. Information PhotoSorter processes

PhotoSorter is an Android photo-organizing application. With your permission, it can read photos and related media metadata that Android makes available to the app, such as media identifiers, timestamps, dimensions, filenames where available, and album or folder information.

The app uses this information to let you browse your library, create groups, organize photos, search local metadata, and optionally prepare on-device visual representations that can be used to find visually similar photos.

PhotoSorter does not currently require a developer-controlled user account.

2. Photos and photo permissions

PhotoSorter accesses photos according to the photo-library permission you grant through Android. Depending on your Android version and your choice, this may be full photo access or access only to selected photos.

The app is designed to treat your original photo library as read-only for its organization features. Creating, renaming, or deleting an app group does not delete the original photos from your device or photo provider.

You can change or revoke photo access through Android settings. If you grant access only to selected photos, PhotoSorter can organize only the photos Android makes available to it.

3. On-device analysis and visual matching

PhotoSorter may analyze accessible photos locally on your device to create visual embeddings or similar derived data. These representations can be used for features such as “Find similar” and user-confirmed visual grouping.

The current app performs this analysis on-device. Photo embeddings, group information, labels, review decisions, indexes, and related organization metadata are stored locally in app-private storage.

Visual matching is probabilistic and may produce incorrect or unexpected suggestions. The app is designed so that important group membership can be reviewed or corrected by the user.

4. Network access and uploads

The current app build does not request the Android Internet permission and does not contain a developer-controlled photo-upload path. PhotoSorter does not upload your photo library or visual embeddings to a server operated by POMF.

Android, Google Play, your device manufacturer, or your photo/cloud provider may separately use network services as part of their own operating-system, store, backup, or cloud-photo functionality. Those services are governed by their own privacy policies and are outside PhotoSorter's direct control.

5. Cloud-backed photos

Some photos visible through Android may be stored partly or entirely by a cloud-photo provider. Whether such a photo is available to PhotoSorter, and whether Android or the provider needs to retrieve it, depends on the operating system and provider.

PhotoSorter does not control the provider's cloud storage, synchronization, retention, or network behavior.

6. Data sharing

PhotoSorter does not intentionally sell your personal information. The current app does not send your photo library, embeddings, group names, or organization decisions to a developer-controlled server.

Google Play and Android may process information related to app distribution, installation, updates, permissions, and device operation under Google's and the applicable platform provider's own terms.

7. Local storage and security

PhotoSorter stores app settings, photo-index metadata, groups, labels, organization decisions, and derived visual data locally in app-private storage. Cached thumbnails or other temporary files may also be maintained by Android or app libraries to provide normal app functionality.

Reasonable technical measures are used by relying on Android's app-private storage and permission model. No security measure can guarantee absolute protection against every possible device or operating-system compromise.

8. Data retention and deletion

App-created organization data is generally retained locally while you continue to use PhotoSorter. You can remove groups or group memberships using the app's controls without deleting the original photos.

You can remove the app's local private data by clearing PhotoSorter's storage in Android settings or by uninstalling the app. Clearing or uninstalling the app may remove app-created groups, labels, indexes, embeddings, and settings, but does not itself delete the original photos managed by Android or your photo provider.

PhotoSorter does not currently operate a developer-controlled cloud database containing your personal photo library.

9. Children

PhotoSorter is a general-purpose photo utility and is not designed specifically for children.

10. Changes to this Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as PhotoSorter's functionality, data handling, third-party services, or legal requirements change. The current version will be published on this page.
